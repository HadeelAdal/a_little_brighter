OUR MISSION

Everyone deserves
a chance to feel better.

A Little Brighter started with a simple idea: art can create something beautiful in your life while helping create access to care for someone else.

The problem

Mental-health care can be financially out of reach. Cost should not be the reason someone has to go without the support they need.

Our idea

We sell original art and useful everyday products and dedicate a clearly defined portion of the proceeds to mental-health support.

Our promise

We want the money to be traceable. As the project grows, we'll publish clear information about contributions, expenses, partners and impact.
